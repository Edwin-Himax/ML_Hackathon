THE PATHSNAP PIPELINE

A route is more than a nearest road.

GPS measurements can drift onto parallel streets or jump between disconnected roads. PathSnap considers nearby road positions, scores them with a trained classifier, then uses directed road connectivity and movement to reconstruct a coherent sequence.

  1. Find candidatesNearby directed road segments for each GPS point.
  2. Score the fitDistance, direction, motion and road context.
  3. Connect the routeHMM/Viterbi balances those scores with feasible travel.

Large gaps and impossible transitions create visible breaks. Aggregate reference metrics are computed separately on the server.
